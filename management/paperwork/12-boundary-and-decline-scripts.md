# Boundary & decline scripts

Use when a lead conflicts with Ashliee’s hard no: **no over-sexualized / erotic / sexually graphic work**.

---

## Short DM / text

> Thanks for thinking of Ashliee. She doesn’t take sexualized or erotic concepts — her book stays fashion, beauty, and culinary. If you have a clean editorial/commercial direction, happy to review.

## Email (firmer)

> Hi [Name],  
>  
> Appreciate the inquiry. Ashliee’s management only books fashion, commercial, editorial, beauty, and culinary work that fits a long-term brand — we don’t accept over-sexualized or sexually graphic concepts, wardrobes, or marketing.  
>  
> If the creative can be reframed within those boundaries, send a moodboard and usage details and we’ll take another look.  
>  
> Best,  
> Triple 8 Media Group

## Internal filter (yes/no before you even reply “maybe”)

Ask yourself:

1. Would we put this on the public portfolio homepage?  
2. Would this still look smart on her feed in 5 years?  
3. Is “sexy” the *point* of the job, or just tasteful fashion energy?  

If (1) or (2) is no, or (3) is “the point,” **decline**.

## Soft redirect

> We can do strong glam, body-confident fashion, and evening looks — just not lingerie/erotica-led concepts. Open to a fashion-editorial angle?
