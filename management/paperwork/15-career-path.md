# Emily-in-Paris path from Sacramento

Emily’s actual job is not modeling. She is a social/brand marketer at a firm that handles fashion and lifestyle clients, and the glamour (events, wardrobe, city) is a byproduct of that seat. Ashliee’s version keeps the same spine: **marketing degree → campaigns she runs → a desk that sends her into rooms**. Modeling, cosmetology, and culinary stay the 80/20 book and the face of the work. They do not replace the degree.

School name and graduation year are not on file. Phases below are relative to “while enrolled” and “after the degree.”

## What “Emily” means in practice

- Owns social and brand work for clients (fashion, beauty, hospitality, wine), not only appears in it
- Spends time in rooms: dinners, mixers, activations, with a brief and a follow-up, not only a comp ticket
- Dress and taste are part of the job; the paycheck is marketing
- The foreign city comes **after** a track record and a sponsor (company transfer, study term, or a hired role). Emily did not move to Paris and then find Savoir

Sacramento equivalents of her client list: wineries and wine-country tourism (already in motion via Wolfe Heights / E2), restaurants and hospitality groups, local fashion and beauty retail, Visit Sacramento / civic lifestyle, small agencies downtown. Bigger fashion desks are in the Bay Area and LA; those are commute, remote, or a later move, not year one.

## Phase 0 — Now (in school)

Keep the current lock: 80% modeling / 20% cosmetology, culinary light, 90-day cash band $750–$1,000, no sexualized work. Portfolio: https://formula38.github.io/ashliee-moore/

Add a marketing lane beside the book:

- One internship or campus role per term: social coordinator, content intern, or brand assistant. Target Sac agencies, a winery/hospitality group, a boutique, or a tourism/civic account
- Every paid or trade appearance (Dîner en Blanc, E2 mixer, future tables) gets a **marketing debrief**: who was in the room, what brand it served, one asset she can show a future employer (recap post, guest list insight, content set)
- Coursework aimed at the seat: social strategy, brand management, consumer behavior, analytics, one writing-heavy class. French only if Paris itself is the city, not just the vibe

Cash stays small and local. The degree is the asset that makes the later salary possible.

## Phase 1 — Last year of school through ~12 months after

Title to hunt: **social media coordinator**, **marketing coordinator**, or **junior account coordinator**.

- In Sacramento that is often about **$45k–$60k** plus whatever modeling still clears. Treat modeling as supplemental, not the offer she negotiates against
- Portfolio shift: 3–5 campaigns **she ran** (brief, posts, results), with modeling stills as supporting proof she can also be the face
- Stay on lifestyle accounts (beauty, fashion retail, food, wine). Decline work that fights the brand lock
- Bay Area hybrid is the first stretch if Sac roles are only generic small-business social. LA is the fashion-density option and a bigger move; it is optional, not required to look like Emily

Milestone spread (same habit as event logging): internship locked before the last semester; job applications start ~4 months before graduation; first coordinator seat within ~12 months of the degree.

## Phase 2 — About 2–5 years after the degree

Title: **brand manager**, **social lead**, or **account manager** on fashion, beauty, or hospitality.

- Typical band once she has campaign proof: roughly **$70k–$100k+** depending on Sac vs Bay/LA and in-house vs agency. Agency teaches the Emily pattern faster (many clients, events, pitches). In-house at one lifestyle brand teaches depth
- Events become **work**: she represents the client, brings a shot list and a follow-up list, and Triple 8 logs them as marketing appearances when she is the talent and as career reps when she is the marketer
- Modeling days drop to selective book work (lookbook, runway, hosting) that feeds the same brands she markets

This is the closest sustainable match to Emily’s day: client work, a point of view, rooms, outfits, and a salary that is not a ticket comp.

## Phase 3 — The city (only after Phase 2 proof)

Paris specifically needs a reason an employer will sponsor her, or a student visa. From Sacramento the realistic order is:

1. **Study or exchange term** in Europe while still enrolled (lowest friction if the school offers it)
2. **US brand or agency with an EU office** — transfer is how Emily got there
3. **Direct hire abroad** after a specialist portfolio (luxury social, hospitality, wine) plus enough French to work

Closer “Emily cities” that do not wait on a visa: San Francisco, Los Angeles, New York. Use one of those if the goal is the job; use Paris if the goal is the city, and start language plus an exchange search during Phase 0–1.

Do not pause the degree to chase a move.

## How this sits next to Triple 8

Modeling bookings, rate card, and trade rules stay as they are. The new lane is a career track, not a new rate-card row, until she is paid to **market** rather than appear.
